About the model. Discrete-time queueing simulation (1 s steps) over a 3×3 signalized grid: Poisson arrivals, saturation discharge of 1800 veh/h per approach, 12 s link travel time, jam capacity of 20 vehicles per approach with spillback blocking, and a 3 s lost time charged on every phase change to both controllers. The AI side runs max-pressure control (Varaiya, 2013), a provably throughput-optimal adaptive policy, with an 8 s minimum and 60 s maximum green. Both networks receive byte-identical, seeded demand. Run node test/verify.js in the repository to reproduce every number deterministically.
